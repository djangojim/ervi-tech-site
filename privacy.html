Privacy

We collect what you send us to file a registration: your name, your email address, your company's legal name, its USDOT number and its fleet size. We use it to prepare and submit the filing you asked for, and we share it with the Unified Carrier Registration Plan for that purpose. We do not sell it.

We do not store card numbers.

To see, correct or delete what we hold, write to support@ervi.tech.
