Services

What we file

  • 01

    Annual UCR registration

    The yearly registration owed by interstate motor carriers, brokers, freight forwarders and leasing companies. We file it for the registration year you need.

  • 02

    Fleet size check

    The government fee depends on the number of vehicles you operate. We compare your count with your FMCSA record before we file.

  • 03

    Renewal reminder

    We tell you when registration for the next year opens.

How it works

Four steps

  1. 1

    Send us your USDOT number and your fleet size.

  2. 2

    We confirm the government fee and our service fee in writing before you pay.

  3. 3

    We file your registration with the UCR Plan.

  4. 4

    You receive the UCR Plan's receipt by email.

Fees

Two lines, one total
